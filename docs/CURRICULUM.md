# SmartyLang curriculum: A1 to C1

Design, review of the first draft and the unit outline: `docs/curriculum/OUTLINE.md`. How to deliver lessons, pictures and audio: `docs/CONTENT-HANDOFF.md`.

Units have as many lessons as their content needs. **Review** lessons teach nothing new; each unit ends with a mission/unit test. `*` = gets a picture. From B1, **Exposure** = words met glossed in that lesson's scene (not tested).

## Beginner (A1)
After this level the learner can handle simple everyday exchanges: greet and introduce themselves, talk about family, food, daily routine, home, clothes, shopping, town, weather, yesterday and travel plans.
