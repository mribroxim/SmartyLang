# Sound credits

All sounds are derived from **Interface Sounds** by Kenney (https://www.kenney.nl),
released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain, no attribution required; credit given anyway).
Source files were obtained from the CC0 mirror https://github.com/Calinou/kenney-interface-sounds.
Files here are trimmed, pitch-shifted/layered into short phrases, peak-normalised and encoded as mono MP3 with ffmpeg.
No Duolingo assets were used.
