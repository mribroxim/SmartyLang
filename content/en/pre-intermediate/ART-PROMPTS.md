# Smarty art brief: en pre-intermediate

Generated by `node scripts/content/art-prompts.mjs en pre-intermediate` from 12 unit files and the room catalogue. Re-run it after content changes. Tracking sheet: `content/en/pre-intermediate/art-list.csv`. Pictures are shared by all languages: an image id that already exists doesn't need drawing again.

**To deliver:** 128 word, 140 scene, 12 room-background, 432 furniture, 10 animation.

## How to use

1. Paste the **style block** first, then the item's prompt.
2. Export at the exact **size** and **file type** of its section, and name the file exactly as listed.
3. Keep one look across everything: compare each new image with `public/room/bed/paw.webp` (furniture) and the Smarty stickers.
4. Send files back with the same names. Missing images simply don't show yet; nothing breaks.

## Style block (paste before every prompt)

> Cute cartoon illustration in the exact style of the "Smarty" app: monochrome only (pure black, white and soft greys, no other colours unless the prompt names ONE accent colour), soft 3D-shaded cartoon look like a glossy vinyl toy, thick clean dark outline around every shape, rounded friendly shapes, gentle top-left lighting with soft shadows, simple and readable at small sizes, playful cat-themed details. Smarty is a small black cat with big round white eyes, black pupils and a white shine dot, short rounded ears, a long curled tail. His friends are cats too, in shades of grey and white. No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.

Every unit unlocks the room that matches its topic. Each room = 1 empty background + 6 furniture slots × 6 designs (the first, "simple", is free; the others are bought with coins). The bedroom already exists.

**Background**
- **Size:** 1920×1080 (16:9)
- **File:** PNG, full background
- **Framing:** empty room seen straight from the front, back wall and floor only (no furniture), the floor line at about 63% from the top exactly like the existing bedroom, keep the centre 1080 px wide band the most important (phones crop the sides)

**Furniture**
- **Size:** 1024×1024
- **File:** PNG with transparent background
- **Framing:** single piece of furniture, slight 3/4 view from the front-left and a little above, standing on an invisible floor, bottom edge of the object touching the bottom 5% of the canvas, no shadow on the ground, matches the existing bedroom furniture
